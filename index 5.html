What is Hatha Yoga?

Hatha Yoga is a traditional branch of yoga that focuses on physical postures, breathing techniques and meditation. The word "Hatha" represents the balance between different energies of the body.

Hatha Yoga helps improve flexibility, strength, balance, concentration and relaxation. It is suitable for beginners and can be practiced at a comfortable pace.

Yoga Asanas

Tadasana

Also called Mountain Pose. It improves posture, balance and body awareness.

Vrikshasana

Also called Tree Pose. It improves balance, concentration and stability.

Trikonasana

Triangle Pose improves flexibility and strengthens the legs and core.

Bhujangasana

Cobra Pose strengthens the back and improves flexibility of the spine.

Padmasana

Lotus Pose helps calm the mind and is commonly used for meditation.

Pranayama Practices

Anulom Vilom

Alternate nostril breathing that helps improve concentration and relaxation.

Kapalabhati

A breathing practice that helps energize the body and improve breathing awareness.

Bhramari Pranayama

Bee breathing technique that helps calm the mind and reduce stress.
